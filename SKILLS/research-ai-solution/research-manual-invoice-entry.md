# AI Solution Research: Manual Invoice Processing

## Executive Summary
Research into automating manual invoice data entry for small accounting firms using OCR and LLMs. The goal is to reduce human error and save up to 15 hours of manual work per week.

## SWOT Analysis
### Strengths
- High accuracy with modern LLMs.
- Scalable solution for firms with increasing volume.
### Weaknesses
- Dependence on clear scan quality.
- Initial setup cost for small firms.
### Opportunities
- Integration with popular accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero).
- Expansion into expense tracking.
### Threats
- Low-cost outsourcing competition.
- Data privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA).

## Competition & Market Landscape
- **Rossum**: AI-powered data localized for large enterprises.
- **Vic.ai**: Focus on mid-sized to large firm autonomous accounting.
- **DocuPhase**: General document workflow automation.
- **Dext**: Strong presence in small firm automated receipt/invoice capture.

## Proposed Business Model
SaaS subscription model based on usage tiers (number of invoices processed per month).

## Challenges
- Ensuring high accuracy for non-standardized templates.
- Building trust in autonomous data entry.

## Ease of Implementation
**Score:** 7/10
**Rationale:** Pre-trained models for OCR and data extraction are becoming commoditized, making the core technology accessible. Integration is the primary challenge.
