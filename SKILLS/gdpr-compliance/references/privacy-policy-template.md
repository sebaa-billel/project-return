## 6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access** — You can request a full export of all personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Erasure** — You can request deletion of all your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Right to Portability** — Your data export is available in JSON format.
- **Right to Rectification** — You can update your account details.
- **Right to Object** — You can object to processing based on Legitimate Interest.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent** — You can revoke analytics consent at any time via the cookie banner.

To exercise any right, contact us at: **[your-privacy@email.com]**

We will respond within **30 days**.
